# Hi, I'm Ángel! 👋

I'm a final-year PhD researcher in Astrophysics at the **International Centre for Radio Astronomy Research (ICRAR)** at **The University of Western Australia (UWA)**[cite: 2]. 

My research focuses on analysing the early Universe using cosmological simulations, specifically trying to figure out how early ($z \ge 2$) massive galaxies form and subsequently stop forming stars ("quench") and the role of supermassive black holes (via AGN feedback) and environment in shaping this[cite: 2].

## What I Do 🌌

- **Simulating Galaxies:** Working with cutting-edge cosmological suites like **COLIBRE** and semi-analytic models like **SHARK**[cite: 2, 3, 4, 5].
- **Bridging Theory & Observations:** Comparing model predictions with observational data from the **James Webb Space Telescope (JWST)** and other major facilities[cite: 2, 4].
- **Building Code:** Benchmarking dark matter halo-finders and tree-building algorithms[cite: 2, 5].
