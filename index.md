### Research Overview

I am a final-year PhD researcher at ICRAR/UWA investigating the emergence and evolution of massive quenched galaxies at high redshift (z≥2) using cosmological hydrodynamical simulations. My work connects numerical predictions from hydrodynamical suites like **COLIBRE** and semi-analytic models like **SHARK** with observational insights from the **James Webb Space Telescope (JWST)** and other major observing facilities, focusing on AGN feedback mechanisms and environmental effects. I have also expertise on running simulations and tools that find and connect dark matter halo merger trees in them.
