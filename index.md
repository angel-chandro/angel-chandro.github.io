### Research Overview

I am a final-year PhD researcher at ICRAR/UWA investigating the emergence and evolution of massive quenched galaxies at high redshift ($z \ge 2$) using cosmological hydrodynamical simulations[cite: 2]. My work connects numerical predictions from suites like **COLIBRE** and semi-analytic models (**SHARK**) with observational insights from the **James Webb Space Telescope (JWST)**, focusing on AGN feedback mechanisms, environmental effects, and dark matter halo merger trees[cite: 2, 4, 5].
